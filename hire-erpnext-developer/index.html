    Send your ERPNext version, affected modules, current custom apps, expected outcome, and timeline to hello@aerele.in. For an integration, include the other system and its API documentation.

  2. Agree the scope and engagement.

    Discuss the relevant developer experience, technical dependencies, acceptance criteria, and availability. Choose hourly work, a dedicated developer with a quarterly commitment, or a fixed-scope sprint.

  3. Build and review the result.

    Review changes against the agreed business workflow. Include permissions, integration failures, and version compatibility in acceptance criteria where relevant. Your functional team or implementation partner owns business-process decisions.

Frequently asked questions about hiring ERPNext developers

How much does it cost to hire an ERPNext developer?

Three models: Hourly (₹3,000–4,000/hr) for quick fixes and consultations. Dedicated Developer (₹7,000–10,500/working day) for ongoing ERPNext development – minimum quarterly commitment, limited availability due to high demand. Development Sprint (₹1,50,000/sprint) for scoped 3-week deliverables. All include full engineering team backing.

What's the difference between an ERPNext developer and an ERPNext contributor?

An ERPNext developer configures and customizes ERPNext. An ERPNext contributor has code merged into the ERPNext source code itself. Aerele has 800+ PRs merged across the Frappe ecosystem. You can review the repository-specific contribution links above to assess relevant experience before hiring.

Does Aerele do ERPNext implementation?

No. We're a core development team – custom apps, integrations, product engineering, and performance optimization. For ERPNext implementation (go-live, data migration, training), we recommend a certified Frappe partner. We often work alongside them as the development team.

My ERPNext is slow. Can you fix it?

Yes. We've taken 8-hour ERPNext batch jobs to under a minute. Most ERPNext performance issues are code-level – missing indexes, N+1 queries, inefficient background jobs – not hardware problems. Our developers know where ERPNext is fast and where it's slow because they contribute to the code.

Which ERPNext modules has Aerele contributed to?

Our team has merged PRs into ERPNext core (accounting, inventory, manufacturing, selling, buying, CRM), HRMS (payroll, attendance, leave management), Payments (gateway integrations), Webshop (e-commerce), E-commerce Integrations (Shopify, WooCommerce). All publicly verifiable on GitHub.
