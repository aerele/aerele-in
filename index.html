FAQ

Common questions

What makes Aerele different from other ERPNext service providers?

Our team are active contributors to the Frappe ecosystem with 600+ pull requests merged into core repositories including ERPNext, Frappe Framework, HRMS, and Payments. When you hire an Aerele developer, you're hiring someone who contributes to the framework your business runs on – not just someone who configures it.

How much does it cost to hire a Frappe developer from Aerele?

Three models: Hourly at ₹2,500–3,500/hour for quick fixes and small features (subject to team availability). Dedicated Resource at ₹5,500–8,000/working day for ongoing development – minimum quarterly commitment, limited slots available due to high demand. Development Sprint at ₹1,50,000/sprint (2 weeks dev + 1 week implementation), scalable to 2x or 3x. All models include backing from our full engineering team.

Does Aerele do ERPNext implementations?

No. Aerele is a core development team, not an implementation partner. We specialize in custom app development, integrations (bank APIs, warehouse robotics, e-commerce), product engineering on Frappe, and code review and performance optimization. For implementations, we recommend working with a certified Frappe partner alongside our development team.

What open-source apps has Aerele built?

We maintain several open-source Frappe apps: india-banking (12+ Indian bank integrations – HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, SBI, Citi, HSBC, and more – under Frappe's Incubation Program), pwa-builder (turn Frappe sites into PWA mobile apps), medusa_integration (Medusa e-commerce ↔ ERPNext), and emSigner (electronic signatures for Frappe doctypes).
