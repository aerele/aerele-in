How we got here

Aerele started in 2019 in Tiruppur – a textile city in Tamil Nadu, India, not exactly the tech hub you'd expect a Frappe core development team to come from. But that's sort of the point.

We began as most Frappe teams do: building custom apps and integrations for businesses on ERPNext. But we kept running into framework-level issues – bugs, missing features, performance bottlenecks. So we started fixing them. Not workarounds. Actual pull requests to the Frappe and ERPNext repositories.

That became a habit. Then a reputation. By now, our team has contributed 600+ merged pull requests across ERPNext, Frappe Framework, HRMS, Payments, E-commerce Integrations, and Webshop. We contribute to 6 core repositories.

Today we're a team of 30+ Software Development Engineers. We don't do implementations – we do core development. Custom Frappe apps. Bank API integrations. Product engineering. Code review and performance optimization. The kind of work where you need someone who understands the framework at the source level.

Our clients include Frappe Technologies, Alfarsi National Enterprises in Oman, Hotset GmbH in Germany, Rang De, eBullion, Liquiconnect, Insteel Engineers, Ajanta Bottle, ApnaKlub, GuruKrupa Exports, and product companies like Stanch.io, AssureAI, Xavica Software, and Ziyana Software who've hired us to build their entire products on Frappe.
